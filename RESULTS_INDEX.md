# Numerical output index

## Finite-cohort theory
- `finiteN_theory_constants.csv`: mean-field fold and asymptotic prefactors.
- `finiteN_lognormal.csv`: finite-cohort statistics for lognormal exposures.
- `bm_parabola_constants.csv`: Brownian-parabola constants used in the asymptotic calculation.

## Exposure weighting and impact laws
- `cusp_vs_tilt.csv`: cusp coordinates versus exposure tilt.
- `impact_law_onsets.csv`: fold-onset feedback for the five normalized impact functions.

## Heavy tails
- `finiteN_pareto.csv`: finite-cohort statistics across Pareto tail indices.
- `pareto_exponents.csv`: fitted IQR and standard-deviation decay exponents.
- `pareto_late_collapse.csv`: late-collapse probabilities.
- `pareto_raw_mu*.npz`: raw Pareto experiment arrays.
- `dominant_holder_event_independent.csv`: event-independent dominant-holder dilution comparison.

## Avalanches
- `avalanches_equal.csv`: equal-exposure avalanche histogram.
- `avalanches_lognormal.csv`: lognormal-exposure avalanche histogram.
- `avalanches_1.5.csv`: Pareto-1.5 avalanche histogram.
- `avalanche_finiteN_sweep.csv`: finite-size simulation/theory comparison.

## Path-resolved dynamics
- `path_size.csv`: cohort-size sweep.
- `path_ramp.csv`: ramp-duration sweep.
- `path_memory.csv`: impact-memory and execution-delay sweep.
- `path_curves_size.npz`, `path_curves_ramp.npz`, `path_curves_memory.npz`: representative trajectories used in Figures 8–9.
- `stalled_ignition_diagnostic.csv`: matched-cohort stalled-ignition measurements.
- `path_model_meanfield_reference.csv`: binary mean-field benchmark and path-resolved transition-field comparison.
